## Description

PrimeTime is a menu-bar time tracker that marks and divides your time exactly how you want. Attach key: value labels to timers — repo: app, type: review, team: platform — and your time becomes queryable data instead of entries filed into one rigid hierarchy. Start a timer in one click, run several at once, then see where the day actually went.

No account. No subscription. No cloud requirement. Everything lives in a local database on your Mac.

Mark and Divide Time Exactly How You Want:
• PrimeTime's flexible label system — inspired by Prometheus metrics — attaches key: value dimensions to your time, so it's queryable across any axis you invent, not filed into one rigid hierarchy of categories.
• Join your hours to what actually shipped: Correlate repo and work-type labels with commits, PRs, and delivered outcomes — time data joined against the your issue tracker's record of what shipped.

Multi-task in the Modern Era:
• PrimeTime gives you multiple concurrent timers, flexible after-the-fact editing, and note-taking — because real work overlaps, gets interrupted, and needs correcting.
• Stay coherent across parallel agent loops - labels and notes leave a trail of breadcrumbs, so you re-orient in seconds and never lose the thread.

Visualize Your Day, Week, Month and More:
• See where the time actually went — across feature work, meetings, and more — in calendar and chart views built from your own labels.

Flexible Editing and Label Review:
• Log View — Review and freely edit timespans after-the-fact: because real work overlaps, gets interrupted, and needs correcting.
• Label Review — Merge drifted keys and stray values. With PrimeTime it's easy to catch these outliers and correct them before they impact downstream.

Quiet in the Menu Bar:
• PrimeTime lives entirely in your menu bar — no Dock icon, no window clutter. Look for the timer icon after launch: click it to start, stop, and switch, and open the full window when you want to explore. An interactive onboarding teaches the label model with realistic sample data.

Your Data is Yours:
• Everything is stored locally on your Mac, and the app is fully functional offline. Sync is optional and goes through a PrimeTime Server you host yourself — bring your own server when you're ready for multi-device or team use. Coming from Traggo? A built-in importer copies your full history, labels, and colors. And your complete, schema-versioned history exports to JSON whenever you want it.
