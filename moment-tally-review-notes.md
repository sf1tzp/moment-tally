Thanks for reviewing Moment Tally — the first App Store submission from this account, but an evolving project: publicly distributed for macOS as open source (AGPL-3.0) via Homebrew and notarized GitHub releases (github.com/sf1tzp/moment-tally), with a product site at moment-tally.com.

WHERE THE APP LIVES: Moment Tally is a menu-bar-only app. After launch there is no Dock icon or window — look for the timer icon on the right side of the macOS menu bar. Click it to start and stop timers; choose "Open Moment Tally" in that menu to open the full window (Log, Calendar, History, and the Tally launcher).

ACCESS: No account or sign-in exists — no registration, login, or account-deletion flows, and no demo credentials are needed. Every feature works immediately, offline, with local data. On first launch the app offers an interactive Tour that walks through the whole app with realistic sample data — the fastest way to see each feature in action.

PURPOSE & AUDIENCE: A time tracker for individuals — freelancers tracking billable hours, and anyone who wants an honest record of where their time goes. Instead of one rigid hierarchy of projects and categories, every span of time carries simple key: value marks (client: acme, recipe: sourdough), so users can slice their history along any axis they invent, run overlapping timers the way real days overlap, and review their days with calendars, charts, and stats built from what actually happened.

USER CONTENT: All data is private time entries stored on the user's own device (optionally in their own iCloud private database). Nothing is shared with or visible to other users, so content reporting and blocking mechanisms do not apply.

EXTERNAL SERVICES: None beyond Apple's own CloudKit. iCloud sync (new in 1.2.0) is optional and off by default: Settings → Sync → "Use iCloud" uses the Mac's signed-in iCloud account (private CloudKit database, end-to-end encrypted). There are no third-party data providers, authentication services, payment processors, analytics, or AI services, and the app never connects to any server we operate — we run no backend at all. For completeness, two Settings screens accept a user-supplied address: a sync server the user hosts on their own hardware, and a one-time history importer for the open-source Traggo tracker. These are advanced options for self-hosting enthusiasts — no hosted instance of either exists, no credentials are needed from us, and the app is fully functional with both left unconfigured.

REGIONAL: The app functions identically in all regions — no regional content, no region-gated features, no server-side variation; only App Store pricing tiers differ by region.

REGULATED INDUSTRIES / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL: Not applicable. The app operates in no regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material; all code and assets are our own or properly licensed.
